# Review Checklist: WebSocket Security, Conformance Test Kit, & Test Suite

Verify each invariant when reviewing changes to `src/litestar_security/websocket/`, `src/litestar_security/testing.py`, or `src/tests/`:

- [ ] **WST-01 (WebSocket Origin validation & startup enforcement)**: Does `RouteCompiler.receive_route` fail startup when a `WebSocketRoute` with a `session_capable` mechanism has empty `allowed_origins` (and reject `csrf_required`), and does `extract_websocket_handshake` reject missing, duplicate, or untrusted `Origin` headers (`4403`, `origin_denied`)?
- [ ] **WST-02 (Connect-token bindings & reserved query-parameter rejection)**: Are digest-only `WebSocketConnectAuthorization` records bound to `(connect_token_id, digest, subject_id, security_epoch, route_name, origin, restrictions, policy_fingerprint, issued_at, expires_at)` with TTL `<= maximum_connect_token_ttl` (`<= 2m`), consumed atomically once, revalidated for epoch/route/origin/policy, narrowed via `restrictions` in `_merge_connect_token`, and protected by unconditional rejection of `RESERVED_QUERY_PARAMETERS` (`4401`)?
- [ ] **WST-03 (Active WebSocket lifetime, revocation, & outage handling)**: Do `supervise_websocket_lifetime` and `WebSocketCloseCoordinator` close active connections once on expiry (`4401`, `credential_expired`), `WebSocketRevocationSource` signals (`4401`, `credential_revoked`), `AuthorizationSnapshotRefresher` / guard denial (`4403`, `authorization_denied`), or revocation/refresh/epoch outages (`1013`, `verification_unavailable`)?
- [ ] **WST-04 (Unimported test-kit boundary `R5`)**: Does `import litestar_security` leave `litestar_security.testing` unloaded and `InMemorySecurityBackend` absent (`tools/check_import_boundaries.py`), with runtime modules free of `litestar_security.testing` imports (`TID251`)?
- [ ] **WST-05 (`assert_security_backend_conformance` & `assert_rate_limiter_conformance` parity)**: Are all store `Protocol` methods and invariants covered by the 15 `StoreConformanceFactories` suites and standalone `assert_rate_limiter_conformance` (`RateLimiter`), implemented in `InMemorySecurityBackend` / `testing.py`, and runnable without optional extras (`argon2`, `pyotp`, `webauthn`, `cbor2`)?
- [ ] **WST-06 (Unit vs. integration vs. typing test boundaries)**: Are all `Litestar`, `TestClient`, and `AsyncTestClient` usages kept strictly inside `src/tests/integration/` (or `src/tests/examples/`) rather than `src/tests/unit/`, and are `assert_type` checks placed in `src/tests/helpers/typing.py`?
- [ ] **WST-07 (Deterministic `FakeClock` & narrow fixture scope)**: Do time-sensitive tests use `FakeClock` instead of sleeps, with only `clock`/`anyio_backend`/`jwt_key_material` in `src/tests/conftest.py`, session-scoped `local_key_ring` only in `src/tests/unit/conftest.py`, docstring-only `src/tests/integration/conftest.py`, and single-module fixtures kept local to their test module?
- [ ] **WST-08 (`_connect_tokens.py` 100% branch coverage)**: Does `src/litestar_security/websocket/_connect_tokens.py` maintain `100%` branch coverage?
