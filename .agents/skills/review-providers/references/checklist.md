# Review Checklist: Token, Assertion, & Federated Identity Providers

Verify each invariant when reviewing changes to `src/litestar_security/providers/`:

- [ ] **PRV-01 (JWT algorithm & claim pinning)**: Are `alg="none"`, forbidden JOSE headers (`b64`, `crit`, `jku`, `jwk`, `x5c`, `x5t`, `x5t#S256`, `x5u`), and algorithm confusion blocked in `JWTValidationConfig` / `PyJWTVerifier`, and are `{"iss", "sub", "aud", "exp", "iat"}` (plus `client_id`/`jti` under `access_token_profile=True`), optional `nbf`, `typ` (`token_types`), `clock_skew`, and `maximum_lifetime` validated against `Clock`?
- [ ] **PRV-02 (Composite bearer dispatch & API-key Bearer prefix exclusion)**: Does `CompositeBearerConfig` dispatch slots deterministically via `BearerSlotSelector` (`issuers`, `audiences`, `token_types`), does `APIKeyConfig(accept_authorization_bearer=True)` keep `_APIKeyCredentialSlot` on `"api-key"` with `AuthenticationRegistry._coordinate_bearer_slots` setting `excluded_prefixes` or wrapping custom Bearer slots in `_PrefixExcludingBearerSlot`, and does `SecurityMiddleware._plan_uses_bearer` inspect `additional_security_schemes` (`APIKeyBearer`) for RFC 9728 challenges?
- [ ] **PRV-03 (JWKS single-flight, `verify_jwks_jwt`, & negative cache bounds)**: Are fresh snapshot reads lock-free, refreshes coalesced under `asyncio.shield`, unknown `(kid, alg)` entries generation-scoped and bounded by `JWKSCachePolicy.maximum_unknown_keys` (`1_024`) clearing when `snapshot.generation` advances, and does `jwks/_verification.py::verify_jwks_jwt` enforce a timezone-aware `now` pre-check before `JWKSProvider.select_key` and `DynamicVerifierCache[PyJWTVerifier]`?
- [ ] **PRV-04 (API key peppered HMAC, dual-header consistency, rotation, & usage)**: Do `APIKeyCodec` and `APIKeyConfig` require a single 32+-byte `pepper`, hash secrets with domain-separated HMAC-SHA256, compare via `hmac.compare_digest` against `APIKeyState.digest`, reject `header_name.lower() == "authorization"` unconditionally, reject conflicting `header_name` + `Authorization: Bearer <prefix>_...` tokens, rotate atomically with bounded `overlap_until`, and flush `BufferedAPIKeyUsage` on `close()` while dropping when `len(_pending) >= capacity`?
- [ ] **PRV-05 (Google IAP assertion pinning & machine/SPIFFE validation)**: Does `GoogleIAPConfig` pin `ES256`, issuer `https://cloud.google.com/iap`, `token_types={"JWT"}`, valid HTTP `header_name` (default `"X-Goog-IAP-JWT-Assertion"`), and exact audiences; does `GoogleIAPClaims.is_service_account` enforce `external_identity is None`, `hosted_domain is None`, and exact `gserviceaccount.com` or `.gserviceaccount.com` domain; and does `GoogleIAPClaims.spiffe_id` reject `external_identity`, `?`, `#`, whitespace/controls, empty or leading/trailing `.` authority, and empty, `.`, or `..` path segments (including trailing slashes)?
- [ ] **PRV-06 (OAuth transaction sealing, PKCE, & redirect policy)**: Are `state` and `browser_binding` HMAC-SHA256 digested in `OAuthTransactionService`, `return_to` and `redirect_uri` validated against `OAuthRedirectPolicy` (relative `/` non-`//` paths or exact HTTPS origins matching `callback_uris`), `pkce_verifier` and `nonce` sealed via `OAuthTransactionProtector` (`AESGCMOAuthTransactionProtector`) in `MemoryOAuthTransactionStore`, PKCE `S256` and OIDC `nonce` bound, and transactions consumed atomically once via `OAuthTransactionStore.consume`?
- [ ] **PRV-07 (SSRF public-IP DNS defenses)**: Do outbound HTTP clients reject non-HTTPS schemes (`follow_redirects=False`, `trust_env=False`), with `OAuthProviderClient.stream_pinned` -> `OAuthProviderClient._pinned_endpoint` resolving DNS and rewriting URL host to the literal public IP with `Host` and `extensions={"sni_hostname": hostname}` across `OAuthProviderClient` and `GitHubOAuthProvider(..., resolver=...)`, and `HttpxJWKSFetcher` / `OIDCDiscoveryClient` (`DiscoveryPolicy`) delegating public-IP DNS and bounded streaming checks to `providers._internal.resolve_public_addresses` and `providers._internal.read_bounded_body` unless `allow_private_hosts=True`?
- [ ] **PRV-08 (`CORE_COVERAGE` 100% branch coverage)**: Are all branches in `src/litestar_security/providers/jwt/*` and `src/litestar_security/providers/oauth/_transactions.py` covered by deterministic offline unit and integration tests?
